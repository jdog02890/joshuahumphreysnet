Our Story

A boat slip, a beat-up kitchen, and a stubborn idea.

The Salty Hook started in 2018 when Mike Calloway — a former line cook from Charleston who'd been running a 28-foot Mako out of Fernandina Harbor — realized he was selling his catch to restaurants for half what they were charging on the plate.

So he leased a tiny spot on Centre Street, took two ice chests, a cast iron, and his mother-in-law's recipe for hush puppies. Six years later, the hush puppies are the same. Everything else got a little bigger.

Local boats only We buy direct from Amelia Island and Mayport captains.
Daily menu If it didn't swim this week, it's not on the plate.
Family owned Mike runs the kitchen. His wife runs the front.
Tip the staff Service charge stays optional. They earn it.
