About

Hi, I'm Iris.

I've been photographing weddings on Amelia Island and along the Northeast Florida coast for the past nine years. Before this I was a documentary photographer in Savannah — which is a long way of saying I notice the small stuff. The hand-squeeze before the ceremony. The grandmother's face when you walk down the aisle. The exact moment your dad realizes you actually did it.

"Photography, at its best, is a way to look at someone the way they wish they could see themselves."

When I'm not shooting I'm somewhere on the water with my husband Pete, two kids, and a very confused dog named Banjo.
