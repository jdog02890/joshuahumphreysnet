Our Story

A 30-seat kitchen on Centre Street.

We pull the freshest catch from local waters, pair it with seasonal produce from nearby farms, and keep things simple. In historic Fernandina Beach, steps from the marina. Come hungry, leave happy.

Every plate tells the story of the people who grew it, caught it, or raised it. That is what coastal dining should be.
