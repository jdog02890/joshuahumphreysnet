Frequently asked.

Can I see a real working demo, or are these just mockups?

Real working demos — not Figma mockups, not screenshots. Every "View Demo" button above opens a fully interactive preview built with the same structure, sections, and styling you'd get as a paying client. Click any card, scroll the menus, try the forms — what you see is what you buy.

What do I actually get when I buy a template?

You get a WordPress site that lives on your own hosting (I'll help you set that up), your own domain, and full ownership. Not a subscription, not a rented site. The price covers the template, customization with your photos and copy, basic SEO setup, and launch. After launch you own it forever.

How is this different from buying a Squarespace or Wix template?

Three things: (1) WordPress is way more flexible and faster long-term — no monthly platform lock-in. (2) Local optimization — these templates are built knowing Jacksonville and Fernandina Beach businesses, with local SEO baked in. (3) A real human in your area builds it. If you're confused, you text me.

Do you build sites outside of these 5 niches?

Absolutely. The configurator lets you spec a custom build for any business. The 5 templates here are just the ones I have a pre-built starting point for, so they're cheaper and faster. Custom builds start at $1,997.

How long does it take to launch?

7–14 days from when you send me your content, photos, and brand info. Most clients are live in 10 days.

What about hosting and the domain?

If you already have hosting, I'll deploy there. If you don't, I'll recommend the cheapest option that won't fall over (usually around $3/month). You buy the hosting and domain in your name — you own everything. I'm not in the middle of your bill.
