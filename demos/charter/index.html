The Captain

Capt. Mike Whitlow.

Twelve years of full-time fishing the Northeast Florida coast. I grew up casting off the rocks at Fort Clinch and never really stopped. Before this I worked the docks in Mayport for a decade, so I know where the fish hold and — more importantly — when to leave them alone.

I run one boat. I take one charter a day. That means you get my full attention, not the rushed energy of a guy trying to hit a quota. We go when the fish are biting, we come back when you've had enough.
