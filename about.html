AboutJoshua Humphreys

Web designer & IT specialist in Fernandina Beach.

I help businesses get online, look professional and actually show up when people search for them. I'm based in Fernandina Beach for local, in-person work across Northeast Florida, and I work remotely with clients anywhere in the United States. If you need a website, I can build it no matter where you are.

I hold a CompTIA A+ certification, the industry standard for IT support professionals, and I take a hands-on approach to every project.

01Background

Local to Fernandina Beach, remote everywhere else.

I moved to Fernandina Beach and pretty quickly noticed that a lot of small businesses here either had no website at all or one that looked like it had not been touched in years. That kept bothering me, so I decided to do something about it.

I'm not an agency and I'm not trying to be one. I genuinely enjoy building things and solving problems, and if I can help a business owner get more customers by building them a solid website, that is a win for everyone. Almost everything I do can be done fully remotely, so I work with clients here in Nassau County and anywhere else in the United States. You do not have to be local to hire me.

03How I work

What you can expect.

No agency layers and no surprises. Just straightforward work from one person who cares how it turns out.

  1. You work directly with me on every project, not a team member or subcontractor.
  2. Pricing is agreed on upfront. No surprise charges when the project is done.
  3. I respond to calls and messages quickly. You will not be waiting days to hear back.
  4. I'm available evenings and weekends to fit around your schedule.
  5. Every project includes 7 days of free support after launch, with flexible ongoing plans after that.
