07FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Text me at (904) 310-4371 and I will get back to you quickly.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is different, so I give flat-rate quotes after we talk about what you need. Most small business websites range from about $750 to $2,500 depending on complexity. Simple sites are on the lower end and larger multi-page sites on the higher end. No hourly billing, no surprise fees, just one clear price upfront.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most websites are completed within 1 to 2 weeks. Simpler projects can be done faster, and larger sites may take a bit longer. I will give you a clear timeline before we start so you know exactly what to expect.

Do you only work with local businesses?

Not at all. I'm based in Fernandina Beach and love working with local businesses in person, but I work with clients anywhere remotely. Web design, Google Business setup and most services can be done 100% online.

What if I already have a website but it needs updating?

I can help with that too. Whether it is a full redesign or a few updates and fixes, I will look at what you have and recommend the best path forward. Sometimes a refresh is all you need.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Absolutely. Every project includes 7 days of free support after launch, and after that I offer flexible ongoing plans for updates and maintenance. I do not disappear after the project is done. If something breaks or you want to add something new, just reach out.

What platforms do you build on?

I work with WordPress, HTML/CSS/JavaScript, Shopify, WooCommerce, Squarespace and Netlify. I recommend the platform that makes the most sense for your business and budget, not the one that is easiest for me. If you are not sure what you need, I will walk you through the options.

What happens after my site launches?

Every project includes 7 days of free support after launch. During that window I take care of any issues, tweaks or questions at no extra charge. After that, I offer flexible ongoing support at straightforward rates, whether you need updates daily, monthly or just a few times a year.

Can you help with IT issues too, not just websites?

Yes. I'm CompTIA A+ certified and handle everything from slow computers and printer issues to network setup and software troubleshooting. Available in person locally or remotely.
