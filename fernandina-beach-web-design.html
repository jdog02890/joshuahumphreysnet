01Why it matters

Web design for local businesses and remote clients.

Walk down Centre Street and you will notice a pattern: half the shops, restaurants and services here either do not have a website at all, or they have one that has not been touched since 2015. Meanwhile, the ones with modern, mobile-friendly sites are the first ones visitors find when they Google "things to do in Fernandina Beach" or "Amelia Island coffee shop."

A well-built website is not a luxury anymore. It is the digital equivalent of having your lights on and your sign out front. If you run a small business in Nassau County and you want to get found, look credible and turn walk-bys into customers, that is exactly what I do.

05Why local

Why hire a local web designer instead of a national agency?

When you hire someone in another state or another country, you are a ticket number. When you hire me, we can meet for coffee, you can hand me a USB with your logo on it and you can actually see the person building your site. If something breaks at 8pm, I pick up the phone.

I also understand the local market. I know which businesses your customers already trust, how people here actually search for services and what makes an Amelia Island site feel like it belongs here instead of feeling like a generic template.
