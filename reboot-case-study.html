Reboot Computers of Jax, rebuilt from the ground up.

A complete overhaul for a Jacksonville computer repair and custom PC business. New design, a better experience for customers and a site that finally represents how good the business is.

01The problem

A business that had outgrown its website.

The previous design was built on a dated template with a generic blue and white color scheme, cluttered layouts and pages that did not do justice to the quality of their work. Key services like custom PC builds and business solutions were buried in crowded pages, and the overall look did not match the reputation they had built with thousands of customers and more than 500 businesses served.

They needed a site that looked as good as the PCs they build.

02Before

What the old site looked like.

A generic template, blue header bars, inconsistent layouts and important information lost in walls of text. The shop pages were completely broken, showing raw shortcode instead of product listings.

Old homepage↗ Old services section↗ Old custom builds page↗ Old repairs page↗ Old about page↗ Old contact page↗ Broken shop page: Desktops↗ Broken shop page: Laptops↗ Broken shop page: MacBooks↗

03What I did

Rebuilt the entire site from scratch.

A new visual identity with a dark, modern color scheme that matches the tech-forward nature of the business. Every page was reorganized with a clearer hierarchy, stronger calls to action and a layout that guides visitors toward contacting the shop or browsing inventory.

  • A dark theme with the existing green and blue brand colors working against a dark background instead of white
  • A redesigned homepage with a rotating hero, trust badges and clear service cards
  • Rebuilt Custom Builds, Repairs, E-Recycling and Business Solutions pages with better structure and visible pricing
  • Improved mobile responsiveness across every page
  • Cleaner navigation with a sticky header, contact button and phone number always visible
  • A better footer with organized links, address and warranty information
