01Local · Fernandina Beach, FL

Fernandina Beach web designer

Fernandina Beach is home. If you own a shop on Centre Street, run a charter out of the harbor or operate a service business anywhere on Amelia Island's northern end, I can meet you in person at your shop, at a coffee spot or at your house. In-person consults are always free, and I know the local market well enough to build a site that fits how people here actually search and buy.

02Local · Amelia Island, FL

Amelia Island web design

Amelia Island gets a huge mix of tourists and locals, and your website has to work for both. Visitors want to know your hours, your menu and whether you have parking before they drive to you. Locals want to know you are still open and what is new. I build sites that answer both, show up in "things to do on Amelia Island" searches and look as professional on a phone as they do on a laptop.

03Local · Yulee, FL

Yulee web designer

Yulee is growing fast, and a lot of newer businesses here either have no site at all or one that has not caught up to how their business has grown. I work with contractors, service providers and small shops across Yulee to build sites that reflect how you actually operate now, not how you started five years ago. In-person visits are easy from Fernandina Beach.

04Local · Jacksonville, FL

Jacksonville web design

Jacksonville is a big, competitive market, which is exactly why a well-built website matters more here. Big agencies will quote you thousands of dollars for a template site. I give you a real custom design, built personally, at a fraction of the price. I can meet in person in North Jacksonville or handle everything remotely, whichever works for you.

05Local · Nassau County, FL

Nassau County small business websites

From Fernandina Beach to Callahan and Hilliard, Nassau County is full of small businesses that deserve a professional online presence without an agency budget. I cover the whole county in person, and I'm only a phone call or text away when you need updates or support. Local businesses helping local businesses.

06Remote · United States

Working remotely with clients anywhere in the U.S.

Most of what I do does not require being in the same room. A website is built on my computer whether you are in the next town over or on the other side of the country. If you are in Texas, California, New York or anywhere else in the U.S. and you want a solid custom website without going to an agency or a template site, I'm happy to work with you remotely. We handle everything over phone, text, email and screen-share.

Remote clients get the exact same service local clients do: flat-rate pricing, fast responses, direct access to me and a real custom build. The only thing you miss out on is the in-person coffee meeting.
